What we considered

Departures, not just presenceEvent exits permit bounded pre-positioning. Normal closing departures and continuing patronage have different timing semantics.
One gathering, retained provenanceExact duplicates are consolidated. Nearby but distinct events are retained. Percentages remain relative to each venue's own peak.
Travel and uncertaintyNow keeps the five-mile boundary and adds a route-time limit. Missing feeds are unknown, not zero activity. Census and income add no new ranking weight.
An outlook, not six hours of empty drivingLater windows compare upcoming opportunities independently; they do not predict passenger destinations.

This is the loaded application policy, not a pasted external review. Python enforces the schema, citations, eligibility, expiry and token limits. A prompt alone is not an enforcement boundary.

Census researchPopulation and housing research retained; household vehicle coverage incomplete. Density, vehicle and income ranking weights remain zero.
